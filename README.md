# More Building Blocks 1 (Minecraft Bedrock add-on)

6 new woods and 6 new stones. Each one has a **full block, slab, stairs and door**.
Shapes and collision work like the vanilla blocks.

| Woods | Stones |
|---|---|
| Maple, Redwood, Ebony, Willow, Ash, Azurewood | Marble, Slate Tiles, Limestone Bricks, Red Granite Bricks, Jade Bricks, Polished Onyx |

## Install
Double-click `dist/MoreBuildingBlocks1.mcaddon`. It imports both packs.
You can also open `dist/MoreBuildingBlocks1_BP.mcpack` and `dist/MoreBuildingBlocks1_RP.mcpack` one at a time.
Then turn on the **behavior pack** in your world's settings. The resource pack is added with it.
Requires Minecraft Bedrock **1.26.20 or newer**. No experiments are needed.

## How the blocks behave
- **Slabs** go in the top or bottom half depending on where you click. Click a slab with the same slab to make a double slab, which drops 2.
- **Stairs** face the way you're looking. They go upside-down if you place them on the top half of a block, and they make inner and outer corners with other stairs. They have real stepped collision, so you can walk up them.
- **Doors** are 2 blocks tall and 3 pixels thick, like vanilla. Right-click to open them. Placing a door next to another door makes a double door.

## Crafting
- 8 vanilla planks (any kind) around a dye makes 8 planks: Maple (orange), Redwood (red), Ebony (black), Willow (lime), Ash (white), Azurewood (light blue).
- 8 stone around a dye makes 8 stone blocks: Marble (white), Slate Tiles (blue), Limestone Bricks (yellow), Red Granite Bricks (red), Jade Bricks (green), Polished Onyx (purple).
- Slabs: 3 in a row makes 6. Stairs: 6 makes 4. Doors: 6 (2x3) makes 3. Stone slabs and stairs can also be made in the stonecutter.

## Rebuilding
`python3 tools/build.py` (needs Pillow) regenerates everything in `packs/` and `dist/`.
All textures are generated by `tools/textures.py`.

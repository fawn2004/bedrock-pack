# More Building Blocks 3 (Minecraft Bedrock add-on)

> This repo also has **Factions Core**, a factions add-on for servers. See [FACTIONS.md](FACTIONS.md).

**12 new woods** and **12 new stones**: 180 blocks in total, plus **18 tree types grown by Minecraft's own tree generators** and **32 ready-made bushes and rocks**.
Each wood has a log, stripped log, wood (bark) block, bark stairs and slab, two leaf colours, planks, slab, stairs and door.
Each stone has a block, slab, stairs and door.
Shapes and collision work like the vanilla blocks.

| Woods | Stones |
|---|---|
| Maple, Redwood, Ebony, Willow, Ash, Azurewood, Walnut, Rosewood, Palm, Ironwood, Ghostwood, Plumwood | Marble, Marble Bricks, Slate Tiles, Limestone Bricks, Red Granite Bricks, Jade Bricks, Polished Onyx, Travertine, Gabbro Bricks, Rose Quartz Bricks, Chalk Tiles, Shale Bricks |

## Install
Double-click `dist/MoreBuildingBlocks3.mcaddon`, then turn on the behavior pack in your world.
Requires Minecraft Bedrock **1.26.20 or newer**. No experiments are needed.

## How the blocks behave
- **Logs** point the way you place them (up, sideways, or end-on). Use an axe on a log to strip it.
- **Slabs** go in the top or bottom half depending on where you click. Click a slab with the same slab to make a double slab.
- **Stairs** face the way you're looking and go upside-down on the top half of a block.
  They make inner and outer corners using the same rules as vanilla, and they also join up with vanilla stairs.
- **Doors** are 2 blocks tall and sit on the side nearest you, like vanilla. Right-click to open.
  Placing a door next to another door makes a double door.

## Crafting
- **Logs:** 8 vanilla logs around a dye make 8 logs. 1 log (or stripped log) makes 4 planks.
- **Planks:** 8 vanilla planks around a dye also make 8 planks.

- **Marble Bricks:** 4 Marble in a 2x2 makes 4. You can also make them in the stonecutter.
- **Shapes:** a slab is 3 in a row (makes 6), stairs take 6 (makes 4), and a door takes 6 in a 2x3 (makes 3).
  Stone slabs and stairs can also be made in the stonecutter.

## Trees, bushes and rocks
### Trees (Minecraft's own tree generators)
The pack's script grows a real vanilla tree using Minecraft's own generator. It then follows that tree from its base and swaps its logs for your wood, keeping each log's direction, and makes its leaves never decay.
Every tree comes out different, just like natural ones. Stand on grass or dirt with space above, then run one of these:

`/function mbb/trees/` + maple_tree, maple_tree_small, walnut_tree, redwood_tree, redwood_tree_pine, ironwood_tree, ironwood_tree_mountain, azure_tree, ash_tree, ash_tree_small, ghostwood_tree, ebony_tree, rosewood_tree, willow_tree, willow_tree_mangrove, palm_tree, palm_tree_mega, plumwood_tree

| Wood | Vanilla tree |
|---|---|
| Maple | Fancy oak (small variant: oak) |
| Walnut | Dark oak |
| Redwood | Mega spruce (variant: mega pine) |
| Ironwood | Pine (variant: mountain pine) |
| Azure | Spruce |
| Ash | Tall birch (small variant: birch) |
| Ghostwood | Pale oak |
| Ebony | Acacia |
| Rosewood | Cherry |
| Willow | Swamp oak with vines (variant: mangrove) |
| Palm | Jungle (variant: mega jungle) |
| Plumwood | Flowering azalea |

You can also type `/scriptevent mbb:tree maple_tree` directly.

### Bushes and rocks (structures)
- **Easiest:** `/function mbb/bushes/<name>` or `/function mbb/rocks/<name>` places one centred on you.
- **Command:** `/structure load mbb:<name> ~ ~ ~` places the structure's corner at your feet.
- **See everything:** `/function mbb/showcase` grows 3 of every tree and lays out all the bushes and rocks. Use it on a flat creative world.

Bushes: oak_bush, oak_bush_large, azalea_bush, flowering_bush, cherry_bush, birch_bush, spruce_shrub, jungle_bush, acacia_bush, mangrove_bush, pale_bush, stemmed_bush, stemmed_bush_large, tall_bush.
Rocks: `grey|mossy|marble|travertine|dark` + `_boulder`, `_boulder_large`, `_rock_pile`; plus grey_spire, dark_outcrop, mossy_ledge.

## Rebuilding
`python3 tools/build.py` (needs Pillow) regenerates everything in `packs/` and `dist/`.
Textures are generated by `tools/textures.py`.
To add a material, add one line to `WOODS` or `STONES` in `tools/build.py`.
